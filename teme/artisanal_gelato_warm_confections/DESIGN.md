## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an indulgent, artisanal, and sensory-rich digital storefront experience. Crafted for an upscale gelateria and fondue atelier, the interface merges high-end culinary craft with warmth and hospitality.

The visual direction leans into **Tactile Gourmand Minimalism**:
- Silky, velvety textures and warm cream-based backdrops mimic organic pastry counters and melted chocolate ganache.
- Deep, appetizing chromatic accents balance indulgence and modern culinary luxury.
- Interfaces avoid clinical stark whites in favor of rich custard tones, warm earthenware accents, and deeply saturated berry and cocoa notes.
- Motion feels fluid, viscous, and smooth, resembling melting gelato or pouring hot fondue.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around sensory culinary contrast: deep tart berry, roasted cocoa, warm caramel, and dairy cream.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#A60321` - Bordô Artesanal):** Brand signature, primary calls to action, active selections, badges, and key moments of delight.
- **Secondary (`#8C5C32` - Cacau / Avelã):** Structural accents, supportive metadata, category chips, and secondary icons.
- **Tertiary (`#D9A577` - Toffee / Caramelo):** Warm highlights, seasonal ribbons, ratings, and active hover tints.
- **Accent Soft (`#D97B66` - Terracota / Pêssego):** Secondary highlights, dietary badges (e.g., vegan/artisanal tags), and notifications.
- **Surface / Background (`#F2E9D8` - Creme Suave):** Default canvas foundation providing warmth and reducing digital glare.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`):** Pure light canvas for clean product presentation and contrast.
- **Text Primary (`#2B1810`):** Ultra-deep espresso tone for maximum legibility on titles and primary body text.
- **Text Secondary (`#8C5C32`):** Nut-toned warm neutral for subtitles, secondary metrics, and captions.
- **Border & Dividers (`rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.15)`):** Low-contrast organic separators that maintain structural clarity without harsh lines.

## Typography

The type scale combines the geometric curves and sculptural terminals of **Outfit** with the balanced, highly legible rhythm of **Work Sans**.

- **Outfit (Headings & Display):** Weights 600 and 700 bring personality and culinary stature to flavor descriptions, menu headlines, and hero narratives. Tight negative letter-spacing gives titles a structured, bespoke presence.
- **Work Sans (Body, Microcopy & Forms):** Weights 400, 500, and 600 ensure crystal-clear readability for product ingredients, customization steps, nutritional notes, and checkout interactions.
- **Text Color Hierarchy:**
  - All display, headings, and high-emphasis body text use `#2B1810`.
  - Secondary details, subtitles, timestamps, and supporting data use `#8C5C32`.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements an 8pt-aligned fluid grid system wrapped in generous margins to reinforce culinary luxury and unhurried browsing.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4-column layout, 16px gutter, 20px margins)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (8-column layout, 24px gutter, 32px margins)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12-column layout, max-width 1280px, 24px gutter, 48px margins)
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Product showcases, flavor pickers, and kit configurators utilize generous internal padding (`24px` to `32px`) to evoke bespoke tasting menus.
  - Interactive touch points on mobile honor a minimum hit target of `48px`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids industrial, cold drop shadows. Instead, it relies on warm, organic ambient glows tinted with roasted cocoa and deep burgundy undertones.

- **Surface Ground (`#F2E9D8`):** The primary cream canvas acts as the base layer.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Product Tiles):**
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.15)`
  - Shadow: `0px 6px 20px -4px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.04)`
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Interactive Modals, Active Flavor Selectors):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.18)`
  - Shadow: `0px 14px 32px -6px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.10), 0px 4px 10px -2px rgba(166, 3, 33, 0.05)`
- **Level 3 (Fondue Kit Builder Floating Cart & Checkout Bars):**
  - Shadow: `0px 20px 44px -8px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.14), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The design system embraces ultra-soft, organic, and welcoming geometry reminiscent of sculpted gelato scoops and molten dips.

- **Corner Radii:**
  - `rounded-2xl` (16px / 1rem): Standard input elements, secondary action buttons, review snippets, and badge pills.
  - `rounded-3xl` (24px / 1.5rem): Showcase cards, fondue kit boxes, sensory taste-profile panels, and modal bottom-sheets.
  - `rounded-full` (9999px): Primary CTA pills, counter chips, flavor selection toggles, and icon avatars.

## Components

All iconography is sourced exclusively from **Google Material Symbols Outlined**, maintaining a 1.5px stroke weight and warm cocoa (`#8C5C32`) or wine (`#A60321`) tinting.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#A60321`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Work Sans` 600, border radius `rounded-full`. Hover transition expands slight ambient warm glow (`0 8px 24px rgba(166, 3, 33, 0.35)`). Active state scales to `0.98`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #8C5C32`, text `#8C5C32`, hover fills with `rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text `#A60321` with subtle underline animation on hover.

### Cards (Gelato Flavors & Fondue Kits)
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90)`. Border: `1px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.15)`. Radius: `rounded-3xl`.
- Padding: `24px`. Image containers inside cards receive `rounded-2xl` and warm overflow clipping.
- Card Footer includes direct quantity modifier buttons (`+` / `-`) shaped as 32px pill containers.

### Chips & Flavor Filter Badges
- Unselected: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.20)`, text `#8C5C32`.
- Selected: Surface `#A60321`, border `1px solid #A60321`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Warm Accent Tags (e.g., "Edição Limitada", "Fondue Belga"): Surface `rgba(217, 165, 119, 0.22)`, text `#2B1810`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 165, 119, 0.40)`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.22)`, radius `rounded-2xl`, text `#2B1810`.
- Focus state: Border color transitions to `#A60321` with a `0 0 0 4px rgba(166, 3, 33, 0.12)` halo.
- Labels sit outside in `#8C5C32`, font `Work Sans` weight 600.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Outer bounds: `rounded-lg` for checkboxes, `rounded-full` for radios.
- Unchecked: Border `1.5px solid rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.35)`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#A60321`, check icon in `#FFFFFF`.

### Kit Fondue Customizer (Specialty Component)
- Multi-step modular selector displaying fondue base (Cacau Puro, Avelã Crocante, Doce de Leite com Toffee), companion fruits, and artisanal dippers.
- Segmented step bar uses filled progress pills in `#A60321` connected with `rgba(140, 92, 50, 0.15)` tracks.
